Dual Fist Arena — Game Info (English)
=======================================

Title:
Dual Fist Arena — Timing Stickman Brawler

Category:
Casual (Action · Fighting · Arcade · Brawler · 2D · Single-player · Browser · Free)

TAGS:
Casual, Action, Fighting, Brawler, Arcade, Stickman, Timing, Combat, Ragdoll, 2D, Single-player, Level-Based, Browser Game, HTML5, Free-to-Play, No Download, Desktop and Mobile, Keyboard Controls, Touch Controls

Short Description:
Walk, jump and punch through 40 stickman arenas. Every opponent telegraphs a cue — punch inside that window and the hit lands, swing early and you eat the counter. Dodge sweeps, break armour, parry overheads, chain perfect hits. Five chapters, one new verb at a time, ending with a boss that changes its own rules three times. Free in your browser.

How to Play:
Side-scrolling stickman brawler on one rule: swing at the wrong moment and you are wide open. Every opponent telegraphs a cue.

Move. Left and right arrows or A and D walk the arena. Position is half the fight. Move speed is 320 pixels per second.

Jump. Up arrow, W or Space. A jump clears a ground swing and a sweep, but an overhead will catch you in the air.

Guard. Hold down or S. Guarding absorbs an overhead. Press it just before a fist lands and it turns into a parry.

Punch. Enter or J. Hit the side that is actually open. On touch screens use the two fist buttons.

Dash. Shift or double-tap a direction. Closes the gap when an opponent backs off. Dash distance is 88 pixels.

The hit math. Attack range is 116 pixels with 26 pixels of contact tolerance. The enemy adds a 26-pixel lunge before the cue. Swing early and you get 460 milliseconds of miss-lock — no more punching while the counter comes in.

Damage. A normal hit takes 18. A boss hit takes 28. Max HP is 100. Starting lives are 3.

Perfect window. The first arena opens at 340 milliseconds and tightens as the chapters roll on. Land inside it for a perfect bonus.

Forty arenas across five chapters, eight per chapter:
- Chapter 1 — Timing. Teaches the cue, then adds dash-ins, low sweeps and armour.
- Chapter 2 — Counters. Turns defence into offence with stagger counters, parries and feints.
- Chapter 3 — Weapons. Blades, hammers, chain blades and energy spears drop mid-fight; armoured squads; a shrinking arena.
- Chapter 4 — Tax. Every punch costs stamina, knockback can push you out of the ring.
- Chapter 5 — Final. Combines all of it, ending with a boss that changes its own rules three times.

Coins. 60 per clear, 10 per level, 5 per kill, 2 per perfect. Shop: gear unlock for 300, upgrade for 500. A rewarded ad grants 300.

Assists. Three per run. Focus widens the next punch window by 220 milliseconds for 6 seconds. A rewarded video refills once per run.

Frame-by-frame animation. Every action plays a full sequence of independently rendered transparent frames. Ragdoll knockback on an 11-point mass-spring skeleton drives the flying, tumbling and grounded collapse.

Free, no install, no account. Read the cue. Hit the open side.
